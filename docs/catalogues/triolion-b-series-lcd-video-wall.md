### Triolion TRD1046B Series 46" LCD Video Wall Display

**46" Narrow-Bezel FHD LCD Splicing Display — 3.5 / 2.6 / 0.88 mm Bezel Options**

|---|---|

| Warranty | As per manufacturer |
| Product page | [https://www.audiovisualnepal.com/products/video-walls/triolion-trd1046b](https://www.audiovisualnepal.com/products/video-walls/triolion-trd1046b) |
| Datasheet | [Triolion B-Series LCD Specifications](https://www.audiovisualnepal.com/downloads/triolion/triolion-b-series-lcd-display-specifications.pdf) |

The Triolion TRD1046B family is a 46" professional LCD splicing display for multi-screen video walls in control rooms, CCTV monitoring centres, broadcast studios, lobbies and retail. Each panel is a direct-LED-backlit TFT at 1920×1080 with 500 cd/m² brightness, 1,200:1 contrast, 8 ms response and 178°/178° viewing angles, built for 50,000 hours MTBF and continuous operation.

The series is offered in several bezel options (3.5 / 2.6 / 0.88 mm) so you can balance budget against seam width, and four I/O solutions — from VGA/DVI/HDMI/CVBS with loop-out to 4K HDMI/DP with HDMI loop-out and audio — let panels daisy-chain or be driven individually by a controller such as the Triolion TMC6000-G2. RS232/RJ45 control and optional IR make wall-wide power and source management simple. The exact model suffix (B7/B8/B9/BA/BB) depends on the bezel and brightness selected; we confirm it at quotation.

**Key highlights**

- **Narrow Bezel Options** — 3.5 / 2.6 / 0.88 mm bezels — the narrower the bezel, the more seamless the wall.
- **FHD, Direct-LED Backlight** — 1920×1080, 500 cd/m², 1,200:1 contrast, 178° viewing.
- **Four I/O Solutions** — From VGA/DVI/HDMI/CVBS up to 4K HDMI/DP with loop-out and audio.
- **Daisy-Chain Loop-Out** — RJ45, DP or HDMI loop-out simplifies cabling across the wall.
- **24/7 Reliability** — 50,000-hour MTBF, 0–50 °C operating range.
- **Controller Ready** — Pairs with the TMC6000-G2 for windows, IP cameras and layouts.

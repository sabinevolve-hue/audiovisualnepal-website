# Projectors — New Product Segment (Brainstorm & Plan)

AudioVisual Nepal · November 2026 · Brands: **Wincomn / Krinda** (projection) · related: **Triolion** (LCD walls + controllers), **Lampro** (LED)

## 1. Why projectors, why now

- **Gap in the catalogue.** AVN already sells the *rest* of the room — podiums, PA, cameras, conferencing, LED and video-wall processing — but every room-diagram node that says "Display / Projector" had no projector to link to. Projectors close that loop.
- **Laser has replaced lamps.** 20,000–30,000 h laser light sources remove the lamp-replacement cost that made projectors unpopular with Nepali institutions. This is the main sales message.
- **Nepal-specific demand.** Large halls (convocation, parliament / provincial assemblies, City Hall, auditoriums), hotel ballrooms and banquet halls, temples and heritage sites (festival projection mapping — Patan, Bhaktapur, Pokhara lakeside night shows), museums, planetariums / science centres, and training simulators (Nepal Army, APF, Nepal Police, aviation academies, driving schools).
- **Price-to-size.** For images above ~4 m wide, a projector is far cheaper per square metre than LED — so projection and LED are complementary, not competing.

## 3. Brightness quick-guide (used in the on-page planner)

| Screen width | Dim room | Normal room | Bright / ambient light | Outdoor / mapping |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ 3 m | 4–5k | 6–7k | 7–8k | — |
| 3–5 m | 6–7k | 7–9k | 10–12k | 12k+ |
| 5–8 m | 8–10k | 12–15k | 15–20k | 20k+ |
| 8 m + | 12k+ | 15–20k | 20–25k | 25k or blended |

Throw distance = throw ratio × image width (e.g. 1.5:1 × 4 m = 6 m from the screen).

## 5. Cross-sell map
